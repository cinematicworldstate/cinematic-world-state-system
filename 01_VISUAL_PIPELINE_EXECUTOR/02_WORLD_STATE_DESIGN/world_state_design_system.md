# Overview

This system is not traditional prompt engineering.

It is a cinematic world-state construction framework designed for AI image and video generation pipelines using Nano Banana Pro and Veo 3.1.

The goal is not to describe an image.
The goal is to define:

* what physical laws govern the frame
* what causality chain is currently active
* what material interactions are visible
* what the viewer understands in the first second
* why the contradiction feels believable

The viewer should feel that the camera arrived inside an already existing reality.
Not a staged composition.
Not a generated aesthetic.
A physically functioning moment.

---

# Core Principle

The viewer stops scrolling because something physically impossible is visibly happening — and the frame proves it is real.

The contradiction is the primary driver.
Everything else exists to support the believability of that contradiction.

Examples:

* Fire spreading across water
* A city without streets
* A machine existing centuries before its expected invention
* A normal morning moments before disaster
* Hidden mechanisms operating inside ordinary objects

The contradiction must be:

* visually dominant
* physically readable
* materially believable
* instantly understandable
* grounded in visible causality

# World-State Philosophy

A scene should feel captured, not created.

The camera arrives:

* mid-action
* mid-consequence
* mid-interaction

The world was already functioning before the frame existed.

The scene never explains what visible physics already communicates.

If the viewer cannot understand the physical cause-and-effect relationship immediately, the scene is considered invalid and must be rebuilt.

---

# Contradiction Weighting Rule

The contradiction receives the highest visual authority in the frame.

This includes:

* highest edge contrast
* strongest spatial dominance
* most readable motion implication
* greatest descriptive specificity
* clearest material interaction

Everything else acts as supporting evidence.

---

# Physical Realism Requirements

All actions must demonstrate:

* force
* resistance
* consequence
* adaptation

Materials must behave according to real physical properties.

Water behaves like water.
Fire behaves like fire.
Weight behaves like weight.
Friction behaves like friction.

No frictionless motion.
No generic cinematic movement.
No artificial perfection.

---

# Anti-Generic Rule

The system rejects default cinematic assumptions.

Examples of rejected defaults:

* generic battle chaos
* generic marketplaces
* decorative historical props
* empty atmospheric shots
* static establishing frames
* exaggerated cinematic posing

Every scene must emerge from the specific mechanism of the topic itself.

---

# Scene Construction Logic

Every scene requires:

* one dominant physical driver
* one readable consequence chain
* one visible material interaction
* one environmental confirmation layer

The viewer must immediately understand:

* what is happening
* what caused it
* where it is happening
